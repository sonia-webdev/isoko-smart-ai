# How to record the Kinyarwanda voice clips

You do not need a studio. A phone is enough.

1. Open `voice_recording_script.csv` in Excel or Google Sheets. Start with **tier 1** (67 clips). Tier 2 (63 spoken prices) is optional.
2. The column **say_this** is my draft of the Kinyarwanda. I am not a native speaker, so if the speaker would say it differently, say it your way and write what you said in the column "what_you_actually_said". Never read out something that sounds wrong to you.
3. Record in a quiet room, phone about a hand's width from the mouth, no music, no fan, no echo (a room with a bed or sofa is better than a bare kitchen).
4. Say each item on its own, calmly, with a short pause before and after. Do not rush; do not raise your voice at the end like a question.
5. Save each clip with the name in **save_file_as** (for example `crop_beans.wav`). If you recorded one long file with pauses, that is fine too: send it and tell me the order. I will cut it.
6. Send me the files (a ZIP is fine) plus the CSV with your notes. Mark any clip you are unsure of in the "problem" column.
7. Consent: the speaker should agree in writing (a message is enough) that the clips may be used in Isoko Smart AI, and say how they want to be credited.

What I can and cannot do: I can cut, level and join the clips and check for silence, clipping and noise. I cannot judge pronunciation, so a Kinyarwanda speaker must listen to the final spoken answers before farmers use them.
