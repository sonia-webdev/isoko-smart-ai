# Isoko Smart AI: a one-page guide for farmers

(Kinyarwanda text below was written with AI help. Have a Kinyarwanda speaker check it before printing.)

## English
1. Open Isoko Smart AI on your phone.
2. Choose your crop (the picture), the market where you are, and how many kilos you have.
3. Read the answer: where to sell, and how much more you could earn after transport.
4. Look at the green, yellow or red label. **Red (Low) means the price is old or rests on one report: call a trader first.**
5. Press "Yes" or "No" under the advice. It helps us improve.

By SMS (works on any phone): send `GURISHA IBISHYIMBO IBIRO 500 NYABUGOGO` (sell beans, 500 kg, at Nyabugogo) to the Isoko number.

## Kinyarwanda
1. Fungura Isoko Smart AI kuri telefone yawe.
2. Hitamo igihingwa (ifoto), isoko uriho, n'ibiro ufite.
3. Soma igisubizo: aho kugurisha, n'amafaranga yiyongera nyuma y'ubwikorezi.
4. Reba akarango k'icyatsi, umuhondo cyangwa umutuku. **Umutuku (Hasi) bivuze ko igiciro ari icya kera cyangwa kiri kuri raporo imwe: banza uhamagare umucuruzi.**
5. Kanda "Yego" cyangwa "Oya" munsi y'inama. Biradufasha kunoza.

Kuri SMS (ikora kuri telefone iyo ari yo yose): ohereza `GURISHA IBISHYIMBO IBIRO 500 NYABUGOGO` kuri nimero ya Isoko.
